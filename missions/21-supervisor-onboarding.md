# Mission: Assign A Researcher Route And Safe Project Environment

## Outcome

A researcher can start without guessing about responsibilities, repositories,
data ownership, storage, compute, AI services, reviewers, or exceptions.

## Why This Matters

Technical instructions cannot resolve missing ownership and access decisions.
Assigning every student the same route also wastes time and leaves role-specific
risks unaddressed.

## Before You Start

Complete the universal core and read the
[Supervisor and Staff track](https://github.com/IDEALLab/onboarding-IT/blob/docs/llm-agent-overhaul/docs/tracks/supervisor-staff.md). Use a
fictional or sanitized project for passport evidence.

## Machine And Shell

**Your computer - web browser or text editor.** This mission records decisions;
it does not grant production access automatically.

## Steps

Complete an onboarding packet with:

1. Project and accountable supervisor.
2. GitHub repositories and minimum permission level.
3. Information owner and classification.
4. NAS, Euler, Blade, or other approved storage locations.
5. Responsibilities and resulting passport tracks.
6. Euler account/share need and reviewer.
7. Approved AI route or explicit restriction.
8. Project end or review date.
9. Handover owner.
10. Any exception, scope, expiry, rationale, and owner.

Select responsibilities from actual work. A title does not automatically imply
Python, AI, Euler, Blade, data-steward, or supervisor access.

## Expected Result

The route is necessary and sufficient, access follows least privilege, every
important information decision has an owner, and exceptions are not indefinite.

## Independent Verification

Ask whether the researcher could begin tomorrow without inventing a path,
account, AI approval, or storage location. Then ask whether unnecessary access
can be identified and removed.

## Evidence To Submit

Complete `evidence/supervisor/onboarding-packet.md` with sanitized categories.
Do not publish student records, access tokens, protected project details, or
private membership lists.

## If Blocked

Do not grant broad access while waiting for a decision. Record the blocker and
obtain the information owner, system owner, or lab owner decision. Use
[lab IT policy](https://github.com/IDEALLab/onboarding-IT/blob/docs/llm-agent-overhaul/docs/policy/lab-it.md) for escalation.

## Understand Before Accepting AI Output

An agent may format the packet but cannot authorize access, classify data,
approve AI services, choose compute shares, or create policy exceptions.

## Finish And Continue

Commit the packet for the final supervisor-track review. The next mission
defines handover and offboarding before departure becomes urgent.
