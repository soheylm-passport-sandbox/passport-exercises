# Mission: Create A Recoverable Project Handover

## Outcome

Another authorized researcher can locate the code, data, environment, decisions,
results, owners, and unresolved risks without using your personal account or
machine.

## Why This Matters

Projects often fail operationally after a student leaves even when files still
exist. A handover establishes ownership, provenance, reproduction, access, and
cleanup rather than transferring an unexplained directory.

## Before You Start

Complete the lifecycle and collaboration designs. Use fictional or sanitized
project categories for the passport.

## Machine And Shell

**Your computer - text editor.** Do not revoke access, delete temporary copies,
or transfer ownership during the assessment.

## Steps

Prepare a packet containing:

1. Repository and released/source revision.
2. Environment and dependency declaration.
3. Authoritative data and result locations.
4. Data owner, classification, and access approver.
5. One representative reproduction or verification command.
6. Known limitations, failed approaches, and open decisions.
7. Temporary copies and planned cleanup.
8. Successor and supervisor acceptance.

## Expected Result

The packet is understandable without private credentials or unexplained local
paths. It distinguishes authoritative records from reproducible intermediates
and names decisions that still require an owner.

## Independent Verification

Review the packet from a successor's perspective. Every required input and
permission has a source, every result has a verification path, and no step
depends on your saved login.

## Evidence To Submit

Complete `evidence/data/handover.md`. Do not publish confidential project
details, credentials, personal data, or full storage listings.

## If Blocked

Record missing ownership or retention decisions explicitly rather than
inventing them. Use the
[project handover lab](https://github.com/IDEALLab/onboarding-IT/blob/docs/llm-agent-overhaul/docs/labs/project-handover.md) and ask the
supervisor to assign the next owner.

## Understand Before Accepting AI Output

An agent may format an inventory but cannot certify that paths exist, access
works, results reproduce, or disposal is approved. A person verifies each item.

## Finish And Continue

The information owner or supervisor approves the packet. The data-steward
endorsement is not granted from a self-written checklist alone.
