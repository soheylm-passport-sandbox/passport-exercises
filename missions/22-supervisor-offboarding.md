# Mission: Plan Handover And Offboarding

## Outcome

Project ownership transfers to an authorized successor, required records remain
recoverable, temporary copies are handled deliberately, and unnecessary access
is revoked without destructive improvisation.

## Why This Matters

Departures create data-loss and lingering-access risk when handover starts after
accounts expire. Offboarding must distinguish project records from disposable
copies and preserve incident or retention obligations.

## Before You Start

Use the onboarding packet and current project owners. Perform only a planning
scenario for passport evidence.

## Machine And Shell

**Your computer - web browser or text editor.** Do not revoke accounts, delete
repositories, or remove data during this assessment.

## Steps

Define:

1. Successor for code, data, results, documentation, and open decisions.
2. Repository ownership and pending pull-request disposition.
3. Durable data verification and temporary-copy cleanup.
4. Tokens, groups, shares, machines, AI accounts, and services to review.
5. Retention or legal/policy decisions and their owner.
6. Completion-record retention and refresher status.
7. Final acceptance by the supervisor and successor.

Revocation follows verified transfer; it is not replaced by sharing the
departing person's credentials.

## Expected Result

The successor can operate without the departing account. Required records have
owners, temporary copies have approved cleanup, and access changes are scoped
and reviewable.

## Independent Verification

Walk through the plan after assuming the departing laptop is unavailable and
the personal account is disabled. No required step may depend on either.

## Evidence To Submit

Complete `evidence/supervisor/offboarding.md` using a sanitized scenario. Do not
store real student completion records or access lists in the handbook.

## If Blocked

Preserve data and access state while ownership or retention is unclear. Do not
perform broad deletion to “clean up.” Use the
[project handover lab](https://github.com/IDEALLab/onboarding-IT/blob/docs/llm-agent-overhaul/docs/labs/project-handover.md) and escalate to
the relevant owner.

## Understand Before Accepting AI Output

An agent cannot approve deletion, retention, ownership transfer, or access
revocation. Verify each system through its responsible human owner.

## Finish And Continue

Human acceptance closes the supervisor endorsement. The private registry keeps
only the minimal versioned completion record defined by policy.
